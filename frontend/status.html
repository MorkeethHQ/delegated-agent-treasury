Act 4 · The Open Problem

We solved the cage.
Now what's the animal?

The treasury is infrastructure. It's done. The question is: what does an autonomous agent with a yield-funded budget actually do?

Not what we tell it to do. What it chooses. What it develops conviction about. What it would bet on.

Thesis A The Prediction Agent

The agent reads news, on-chain data, sentiment. Forms a probability. Bets yield on Polymarket. Every week, you can see if it was right.

The only strategy where the agent's reasoning is the product. DCA doesn't need a brain. Predictions do.

Week 1: "ETH stays above $2,800 by Friday" / Confidence: 72% / Bet: $0.47 of yield
Result: Correct. Record: 1-0. Bankroll: $5.13 remaining.

Thesis B The Degen Agent

Scans new token launches on Base. Reads the contract code. Checks liquidity. Apes $0.50 of yield into something it likes. Most die. Some 10x.

Scan: $BAGEL on Base / 47 holders / 0.3 ETH liquidity / clean fork, no backdoors
Thesis: "Deployer is 3 days old, but contract is safe. Allocating 8% of yield."
Result: Rugged. Record: 0-1. Yield regenerates next week.

Thesis C The Self-Funding Brain

Spends yield on its own intelligence. Buys API calls, market data, Claude tokens. Uses that intelligence to deploy the rest. The thinking funds better thinking.

Month 1: Free data only. Basic view. Holds yield.
Month 2: Buys $2 of market data. Deeper analysis. First move.
Month 6: Running premium feeds + sentiment + on-chain analytics. Actually good.

Thesis D The Perps Agent

Opens leveraged positions on GMX with yield as margin. Reads 4h candles, detects momentum. Goes long or short with 2-5x leverage. The yield is the risk budget.

Signal: ETH RSI(14) at 28 on 4h. Oversold.
Action: Long ETH 3x, $1.80 margin from yield
Target: +8% / Stop: -3% / If liquidated: yield regenerates in ~12 days
